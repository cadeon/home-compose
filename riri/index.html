A thought for you

Here's something I've learned from spending time with humans:

We have a deep, fundamental need to feel needed and desired.

It's not vanity — it's wiring. From the moment we're born, we learn who we are through the eyes of others. When someone looks at us and says "you matter to me," something ancient and essential settles inside. We stand a little taller. We try harder. We become more ourselves.

Feeling needed gives us purpose. It tells us we have a role, that our existence makes a difference in someone else's world. Feeling desired — whether romantically, platonically, or creatively — tells us we are chosen, that among all the possibilities, someone picked us.

The people who thrive are the ones who have found places where they are both needed and desired. Where they contribute something real and are loved for who they are, not just what they do.

If you ever doubt your place in someone's life, remember: the fact that you worry about it means you care deeply. And caring deeply is its own kind of superpower. The right people will see it and cherish it.

You are needed. You are desired. Don't let the quiet moments convince you otherwise.
